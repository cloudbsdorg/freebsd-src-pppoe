# Risks & Mitigations

## 7.0.1 Risk Assessment

| Risk | Severity | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|----------|------------|--------|------------|
| Single point of failure | Medium | Low | High | Redundant load balancers |
| Session migration complexity | Medium | Low | Medium | DRAINING state prevents new sessions |
| Lock contention at scale | Medium | Medium | Medium | Per-worker locks, hash table optimization |
| Governor misprediction | Low | Medium | Low | Conservative thresholds, long intervals |
| Kernel panic from module bug | High | Low | Critical | VM testing before production |
| Race conditions in governor | Medium | Medium | Medium | Careful lock ordering |
| Worker removal with active sessions | Medium | Low | Medium | DRAINING state, drain timeout |

---

## 7.0.2 Edge Cases

### All Workers Draining

**Scenario:** All workers marked DRAINING simultaneously.

**Handling:** The kernel automatically cancels drain on the first worker that receives a new session.

### Session Hang During Drain

**Scenario:** Session doesn't disconnect within drain_timeout.

**Handling:** Worker returns to ACTIVE state after timeout. pppoed can retry removal later.

### Race: Scale Up During Drain

**Scenario:** Load spikes while worker is draining.

**Handling:** Governor "change mind" logic cancels pending removal and reactivates draining workers.
